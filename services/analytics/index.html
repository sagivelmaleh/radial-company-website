Analytics & BI

Make the numbers useful.

Create a shared view of performance and a clearer way to answer the questions behind it.

Discuss your reporting needs

Move from reporting activity to decision support.

More dashboards do not always mean more clarity. We start with the people using the information: what they need to decide, what they need to understand and where the current reporting falls short.

Build a common language for performance.

  • Define

    Agree on metrics, calculation rules, owners and decision needs.

  • Design

    Shape reports and dashboards around real user workflows.

  • Adopt

    Review with users, refine the experience and document the meaning behind the numbers.

More than a finished dashboard.

A scoped engagement may include a metric dictionary, a reporting model, dashboard designs, implemented reports and guidance for users and maintainers. The measure of usefulness is agreed with the people who will use the work.

Questions
