Clarity is the starting point.

The value of data does not come from collecting more of it. It comes from understanding what matters, building the right foundations and helping people put the result to use.

Principles

  • Start with the question.

    Understand the decision before choosing the solution.

  • Make complexity understandable.

    Explain choices, assumptions and trade-offs in language the whole team can use.

  • Build for ownership.

    Treat documentation, maintainability and handover as part of delivery.

  • Keep the outcome in view.

    Review the work against the agreed purpose, not only the technical task list.
