Discuss your challenge
  1. 01 / Understand

    Agree on the question.

    We listen to the people closest to the challenge, review the current situation and define what a useful outcome would look like.

    Output: A shared problem statement, initial scope and success criteria.

  2. 02 / Design

    Make the choices explicit.

    We map the data, workflows and dependencies, then agree on a delivery approach that fits the context.

    Output: A proposed solution, a prioritized plan and clear responsibilities.

  3. 03 / Build

    Deliver in focused steps.

    We turn the plan into working increments and review them with your team. Feedback helps keep the work tied to the original need.

    Output: Tested deliverables, review notes and documented decisions.

  4. 04 / Enable

    Make the work usable beyond delivery.

    We document the solution and agree how your team will use, maintain and develop it.

    Output: Handover materials, ownership and a plan for the next step.

A working relationship with fewer unknowns.

Scope, responsibilities and review points are agreed together. When assumptions change, the plan should change with them.
